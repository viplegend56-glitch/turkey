        Why Turkeycoin

        On Thanksgiving, food banks and meal programs have their busiest day of the year, and a lot of families still go without. A memecoin can't fix that on its own. But the fees it makes can go somewhere useful: to a different charity every 15 minutes, all Thanksgiving Day, with a receipt for every donation.

        How it works

        Four steps, on repeat.

        They run every 15 minutes, all Thanksgiving Day.

        Step 1 of 4

        Trade $TURKEY

        Every trade generates creator rewards. How much, and which wallet receives them, is public.

        Step 2 of 4

        Rewards pile up

        They collect in the Turkeycoin donation wallet, live on this page as the vault.

        Step 3 of 4

        The line moves

        It's automated. Every 15 minutes, the next charity in line gets its turn: the ones on donate.gg first, then the rest, by card.

        Step 4 of 4

        A receipt lands

        Every donation gets a receipt on the site, on that charity's own page. No receipt, no count.

        Buying $TURKEY is not a donation.Only creator rewards that settle to an eligible charity count as donations. That's the whole rule.

        Donation receipts

        One receipt per handoff.

        Collected, processing and delivered money are counted separately. Open any donation on Solscan and check it yourself.
